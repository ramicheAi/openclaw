# UNDERTOW — Chapter 3: "Still Heart"

### Manga/Webtoon Adaptation (continues directly from [UNDERTOW-MANGA-CH2.md](UNDERTOW-MANGA-CH2.md)) — episode beats per [UNDERTOW-S1-BEATSHEET.md](UNDERTOW-S1-BEATSHEET.md), Episode 3: "Still Heart," from [UNDERTOW-EP3-SCRIPT.md](UNDERTOW-EP3-SCRIPT.md).

Format note: vertical-scroll webtoon, full color per visdev §1 palette-by-depth, matching
Chapters 1 and 2's conventions exactly. Panels are numbered sequentially within this chapter
and read top-to-bottom in one continuous canvas per scene, gutter width doing narrative work
(a wide gap = a held breath). This chapter's structural thesis is the reverse of Chapter 1's:
Chapter 1 used the scroll to *become* the drowning. Chapter 3 starts a **LONG VERTICAL
DESCENT** the reader has been trained for two chapters to expect — and amputates it after two
panels. The chapter's signature image (Panel 51) is then deliberately the widest, shallowest
panel in the series so far: there is nowhere to scroll. The medium itself refuses to go down,
exactly the way Kai's body does. Chapter 2 was dry on purpose; Chapter 3 is wet and gets
nowhere, on purpose.

Character-design lock: Kai remains drawn per his visdev sheet — a mixed Black boy, deep brown
skin, tightly-coiled blue-black hair falling toward his eyes, his mother's shell-cord bracelet
always on his wrist, **ordinary dark brown eyes in every panel of this chapter** (he never
submerges — his face goes into a basin and never goes under in the pool — so the deep-blue
Undertow glow does not appear once). His heritage is shown, never stated, and no panel
dialogue remarks on it. Bo (heavyset, sun-weathered, always fully clothed — cream cable-knit
sweater, club jacket, and at poolside a long-sleeved club training top over his jammers),
Mirei (bob with the one long asymmetric strand, **prescription glasses on her face in every
single panel including the basin drill**, navy tracksuit, MANAGER armband, stopwatch), Gouda
(graying topknot, heavy jaw, ordinary dark eyes, rumpled tracksuit, **never grinning, not once,
not even at the fish stall**), and Ren (immaculate, blue-black tight undercut, silver-gray eyes
on land) hold their designs exactly per
[UNDERTOW-CHARACTER-CANON.json](UNDERTOW-CHARACTER-CANON.json). No character in any panel
wears a mask, tank, regulator or any other breathing apparatus — every held breath in this
chapter, as in every chapter, is a human being holding their own air.

Palette key used throughout (visdev §1, unchanged): **Chlorine Day** (cyan `#59C7E3`/tile
white `#F2F7F7`/sun gold `#FFD98A`), **Green-Gold** (sea green `#2E8B74`/dawn gold `#E8B84B`),
**Violet Fade** (deep violet `#3B2E63`/steel blue `#2F4A6E`), **Bioluminal** (abyss navy
`#0B1026`/glyph teal `#3EF2C4`), **Absence** (true black `#05060A`). This chapter opens in a
deliberately colorless documentary grey, spends its middle in Chlorine Day and Green-Gold,
touches Violet Fade and one single Bioluminal note for exactly two panels (49–50), and closes
in Old Build sepia-and-blue.

## Chapter stats
- **83 numbered panels** (+ chapter cover splash), holding the series' structural grammar:
  cold-open bridge → title card → three Acts → a warm table button → an intercut end card →
  a post-credits stinger.
- **One LONG VERTICAL DESCENT (#3, Panels 49–50) — deliberately amputated.** It is the third
  in the series count (Chapter 1 had two; Chapter 2 had none by design), it is memory rather
  than event, and it is cut off mid-scroll by the horizontal jolt of Panel 51. The reader is
  taught to expect a fall and then denied one, which is the chapter's whole subject.
- Signature panel: **#52**, the shallow-end relapse — built as the conscious anti-splash to
  Chapter 1's pool-floor splash (#46) and Chapter 2's dock stillness (#62). It is the widest,
  shallowest panel in the series so far because the format itself is refusing to descend.
- **No Undertow eye-glow on Kai anywhere in this chapter** — he goes face-first into a basin
  and never submerges in the pool, so the rule is never at risk. The chapter's only glow is
  Ren's Kuroshio silver at Panel 80, and it appears strictly underwater and vanishes at the
  surface, per canon.
- The Water's Rules are dramatized twice and violated zero times: Bo's dock recitation from
  Chapter 2 is restated by the adult in the room (Panel 43), and the end card's final image is
  Ren — the most gifted swimmer in the show — being watched by an attendant because the rule
  applies to him too (Panel 79).
