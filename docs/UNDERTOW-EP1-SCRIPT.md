# UNDERTOW — Episode 1: "Anchor" (Pilot Teleplay, First Draft)

~22 minutes. From the outline in [UNDERTOW-ANIME-BIBLE.md §12](UNDERTOW-ANIME-BIBLE.md).
Format: anime screenplay conventions, loose on the page for readability. All timings
approximate. Draft 1 — written to be redlined.

---

## COLD OPEN (2:30)

**EXT. OPEN SEA — NIGHT — EIGHT YEARS AGO**

*Black water, black sky, a horizon you can feel but not see. Swells like breathing.
Over black, a woman's voice — old, unhurried, reciting:*

> **GRANDMOTHER (V.O.):** Deep calls to deep… in the roar of your waterfalls. All your
> waves and breakers have swept over me.

*A small fishing boat, anchored. On deck, a MAN — DAIGO ISOZAKI, 30s, built like a
statement — strips off his jacket, eyes fixed on something below the surface. Far
down in the black: a LIGHT. Faint. Patient. Wrong.*

*Behind him, half-wrapped in a blanket, a boy — KAI, age 7 — pretends to sleep,
watching through his lashes.*

**DAIGO** *(quiet, to the water, like answering a question we didn't hear)*: …All
right. I hear you.

*He goes over the side without a splash — a knife into silk. Hold on the empty deck.
The rope lamp swinging. Kai's eyes open.*

**KAI (age 7):** …Dad?

*Beat. The boy climbs the gunwale — small hands, bare feet on wet wood —*

*— and jumps.*

**UNDERWATER.** *The most beautiful and most terrifying water ever animated: moonlight
in shafts, blackness below, the tiny boy sinking through it, air spilling from his
mouth in silver ropes as he claws downward, after the fading light. His father's
silhouette far below, descending toward the LIGHT — and for one frame, only one, the
light resolves into an enormous, ancient EYE opening. The boy screams. Bubbles.
Black.*

*Silence. Then — muffled, distant — his own heartbeat. Slowing. Slowing.*

**TITLE CARD — white on black, one word: UNDERTOW** *(逆流)*

## ACT ONE (6:00)

**INT. MUNAKATA HIGH SCHOOL — CLASSROOM — DAY (PRESENT)**

*KAI ISOZAKI, 15 — lean, guarded, sleeves too long — stares out the window at the one
thing the classroom overlooks: the school POOL, glittering, chlorine-blue. A PE
schedule is being read aloud. The word "swimming" lands. Kai's hand tightens on his
pen. Laughter ripples somewhere behind him.*

**BOY (O.S.)** *(stage whisper)*: Better hand out life jackets. Anchor's in this class.

*Kai doesn't turn around. He's heard it for eight years.*

**MONTAGE — "THE KID WHO SITS OUT" (0:45):** Kai on the pool deck in full uniform
while classmates swim; Kai with a doctor's note the PE teacher doesn't even read
anymore; a trophy case in the school lobby — old golds, a framed photo of a national
podium, and a plaque with the name **ISOZAKI D.** that Kai walks past with his eyes on
the floor.

**EXT. HARBOR — DAWN — ESTABLISHING THE CREW-TO-BE**

*Intercut, wordless, scored to one piece of music:*

- *BO FUJII, 16, enormous and gentle, hauls nets on his family's boat, breath pluming,
  singing badly and happily to himself. He can hold a note — and his breath —
  absurdly long.*
- *MIREI SANDA, 15, in a federation club tracksuit, stands outside a natatorium at
  dawn — then turns around and walks away, leaving her kickboard leaning on the door.*
- *In the school equipment room, COACH GOUDA, 44, asleep in a folding chair among
  deflated volleyballs, an Olympic gold medal being used as a bottle-opener on the
  shelf behind him.*

**INT. MUNAKATA HIGH — HALLWAY — DAY**

*A noticeboard. A hand pins up a plea, hand-lettered: "SWIM CLUB NEEDS ONE (1) MORE
MEMBER BY FRIDAY OR POOL GOES TO WATER POLO. PLEASE. ANYONE. — Fujii."*

*Bo steps back from the board, hopeful. The hallway crowd flows past without
stopping. His face does the thing big friendly faces do when nobody's coming.*

## ACT TWO (6:30)

**INT. SCHOOL STAIRWELL — DAY**

*Kai eats lunch alone on the landing. A shadow falls over him. Bo — holding two
melon breads, offering one.*

**BO:** You're Isozaki, right? Kai?

**KAI** *(wary)*: …If this is about the anchor thing—

**BO:** It's about the club. We need a fourth by Friday or they drain our pool for
water polo guys. *(beat, earnest)* You don't have to swim fast. You don't have to
swim good. You just gotta swim *with us*.

**KAI:** I don't swim at all.

**BO** *(not missing a beat)*: Great — so no bad habits!

*Despite himself, almost, Kai huffs — the closest thing to a laugh we've seen from
him. Then he sees, over Bo's shoulder, the pool through the stairwell window. The
almost-laugh dies.*

**KAI:** Ask someone else.

**BO** *(quiet, honest)*: I asked everyone else.

*That lands — one written-off kid recognizing another. Hold on Kai.*

**INT. FACULTY OFFICE — DAY**

*Gouda, feet on desk, is being lectured by the VICE PRINCIPAL about "the pool
situation." He spots something out the window mid-scolding: Kai, standing at the pool
fence, fingers hooked in the chain-link — the posture of a man at a cliff edge, not a
fence.*

**GOUDA** *(interrupting, still watching)*: …What's that kid's name?

**VICE PRINCIPAL:** Him? That's the Isozaki boy. Doesn't swim. *(dry)* You of all
people should know the name.

*Gouda's feet come off the desk. Slowly.*

**EXT. POOL — AFTER SCHOOL — THE FORCED FIRST PRACTICE**

*Friday. The club's last chance. MIREI is there — clipboard, stopwatch, brand-new
"MANAGER" armband, expression of someone strictly not swimming. Bo beams. Kai stands
on the deck in borrowed jammers like a prisoner at the wall. Gouda leans on the far
fence, pretending not to watch, watching.*

**MIREI** *(checking a form)*: Isozaki… Kai. Fourth member. You know we have to log
one timed length for the registration to count. One length. Any stroke.

**KAI:** …Any stroke.

**MIREI:** Doggy paddle has precedent.

*Kai walks to the block like it's a gallows. The water throws light on his face.
SOUND drops away — we're inside his head: his heartbeat, accelerating. 90. 110. The
water's surface becomes, for one flash-frame, night-black. The EYE. 130.*

**BO** *(soft, from lane-side)*: Hey. Kai. Look at me, not the water.

*Kai looks. Bo grins, taps his own chest — slow breaths, showing him. In. Out. Kai
steps onto the block. The whole world is the six-beat of a starting buzzer—*

*—BZZZT. Kai doesn't dive. He flinches — feet slip —*

*— and he FALLS. Sideways, graceless, a slap of limbs. Someone's phone is already
out. Laughter from beyond the fence, where a knot of students has gathered to watch
the famous Anchor.*

**UNDERWATER.** *Panic, rendered honestly: thrash, bubbles, the muffled roar. Kai's
scream costs him his air. He sinks. The laughter from above bends into whale-song
distortion and fades. His heartbeat: 150 — 140 —*

*And then the show plays its first card. The water… settles around him. The light
shifts — chlorine-blue going deep-ocean cathedral. His heartbeat on screen: 90. 60.
40. His clawed hands drift open. His eyes, squeezed shut, open. He is sitting on the
pool floor, cross-legged in the wavering down-light — comfortable — and around him
the water is paying attention: dust motes and bubbles all drifting subtly toward him,
a pool-sized field of quiet gathered on one boy.*

**34 BPM.**

*Somewhere in the water, almost below hearing: a low, vast, gentle tone. Like a whale.
Like a name.*

**ON THE DECK:** *Twelve seconds. Thirty. Mirei's stopwatch running, her voice rising
as she calls it. Bo hits the water. Gouda has gone bolt upright off the fence, and
his face — the face of a man who has seen this exact stillness once before, and it
cost him everything — has gone gray.*

**GOUDA** *(a whisper)*: …No. Not that. Not *him*.

## ACT THREE (5:30)

**EXT. POOL DECK — MINUTES LATER**

*Kai, hauled out by Bo, wrapped in a towel, coughing — surrounded by the wrong kind of
crowd. Phones. Murmurs: "four minutes—" "no way, the timer says—" "Anchor sank AGAIN."
Mirei stares at her stopwatch like it lied to her: 4:07.*

*The PE teacher disperses everyone. The laughter drains away. Kai sits, soaked,
humiliated, staring at the pool — and the pool, innocent and blue, stares back.*

**BO** *(sitting down beside him, gentle)*: You okay?

**KAI** *(long silence — then, not to Bo, to the water)*: …It didn't want me to drown.

**BO:** What?

*Kai stands. Something has torn open in him — eight years of shame with something
new and enormous underneath it. He speaks to the pool, to the sea beyond it, voice
shaking and getting louder:*

**KAI:** You took him. You took my dad, and you ruined my life, and now you — *what*,
you *want* me? *(a raw beat; then, quieter and infinitely more dangerous)* Fine.
Then I'm coming. You hear me? **I'm going to swim to the bottom of the sea, and I'm
going to bring my dad home.**

*Silence on deck. Then — snickers from the last stragglers by the fence. "The bottom
of the sea." "Anchor's finally cracked." The laughter spreads —*

*— except three faces. Bo, who is not laughing at all. Mirei, looking from her
stopwatch to Kai with the expression of a scientist whose data just got interesting.
And beyond the fence: a TRANSFER STUDENT we haven't met — beautiful, cold, a black
tracksuit with a small silver crest like a wave swallowing a torii gate — REN KUROSE,
watching Kai with an intensity nothing on this deck has earned.*

**REN** *(under his breath, to no one)*: …So it wasn't extinct.

**INT. EQUIPMENT ROOM — DUSK**

*Gouda, alone, digs through a footlocker: old photos — a younger, sharper Gouda on a
podium; the same Gouda, arm around ANOTHER MAN, both in strange dark second-skin
suits, standing in front of what a casual viewer will assume is a cave mouth and a
returning viewer will recognize as Old Build architecture. His hand hovers over the
second man's face. He closes the locker without taking anything.*

**GOUDA** *(to himself)*: Four minutes. *(beat)* Forgive me, Daigo. I'm not going to
train your son.

*Off his face — a man who already knows he's lying.*

**EXT. KAI'S HOME — NIGHT**

*A small house near the harbor. GRANDMOTHER — the cold-open voice — sets a third
place at the table out of habit, then quietly takes it away. Kai in the doorway,
hair still damp.*

**GRANDMOTHER:** You swam.

**KAI:** I sank.

**GRANDMOTHER** *(a small, unsurprised smile, turning back to the stove)*: Mm. So did
Jonah. *(beat)* Wash your hands.

*Kai stands there a second with that. Then goes to wash his hands. WARMTH — the first
truly warm frame of the episode: two people, a small table, grace said over fish.*

**END CARD BEAT — EXT. THE SEA — NIGHT**

*The camera leaves the little house's window, crosses the dark harbor, and keeps
going — out, and down. Sunlit black. Twilight black. True black. Far below, dim as a
memory: bark-glyphs of bioluminescence spiral down a shape too vast to parse — a
TRUNK. Down. Down. The glyphs end at roots gripping something geometric, patient,
sealed. One long, low tone in the deep — the same tone from the pool.*

**CUT TO BLACK. CREDITS.**

## POST-CREDITS (0:30)

**INT. LISTENING STATION — LOCATION UNKNOWN — NIGHT**

*Cold room, old machines: brass-and-crystal instruments that hum without power —
Old Build tech, though no one says so. A TECHNICIAN in a wool coat frowns at a
waveform crawling across a resonance drum. She lays a ruler against it. Checks a
logbook: the last matching entry is dated fifteen years ago. Signed: a name we can't
quite read — except the first character of ISOZAKI.*

*She picks up a phone that has no dial.*

**TECHNICIAN:** …It's awake.

**SMASH TO BLACK.**

## Writer's notes for draft 2 (things to decide on the redline)

1. **The eye in the cold open** — currently one frame. Option: cut it entirely and
   save any visual of the deep's attention for episode 3; scarier, slower burn.
2. **Ren's line** ("So it wasn't extinct") — flags the mystery hard in ep 1. Option:
   he says nothing, and we only get his stare; colder, more AoT.
3. **The 4:07** — is the number said aloud (clean fandom hook) or only seen on the
   stopwatch (subtler)? Currently: seen, then half-said before Mirei is cut off.
4. **Grandmother's Jonah line** — currently the episode's only overt scripture beside
   the V.O. Keep the restraint; her faith should be seasoning in ep 1, load-bearing
   by ep 5 (per elements §4 guardrail).
5. **Luna is absent by design** — she enters in Arc 2 with the hidden world. Confirm
   we're comfortable holding a main cast member until ep 7.
