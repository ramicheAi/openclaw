# UNDERTOW — Step Goals (the order of sequence)

The master goal lives in [UNDERTOW-GOAL.md](UNDERTOW-GOAL.md) (the ten dimensions and
the gauntlet). This file breaks the road into **ordered steps, each with its own goal
card**: what it's for, what "done" means, who does it, and which mastery dimensions it
gets judged against. Work the steps in order — each one's output is the next one's
input. Current step is marked ▶.

---

## ▶ Step 1 — Lock the Canon

**Goal:** every creative pillar approved by the creator, so nothing downstream gets
rebuilt on sand.
**Who:** you (reading/redlining), me (applying changes through the gauntlet).
**Tasks:** read the remaining canon docs (lore codex — especially the §5 endgame — beat
sheet, swim codex); redline the pilot script's five flagged decisions; approve or veto
the big locked answers (Daigo's fate, Nakaru's ending, Kai's permanent price).
**Done when:** you've said "locked" on bible + elements + lore codex + beat sheet, and
the pilot script is at draft 2 with your notes in.
**Judged on:** dimensions 1, 5, 7, 9.

## Step 2 — Own It (the paperwork hour)

**Goal:** the IP legally yours before any collaborator or platform touches it.
**Who:** you (filings), me (prep documents on request).
**Tasks:** trademark search on UNDERTOW (animation/comics classes; decide title vs
logo-identity); copyright-register the canon set as one unpublished literary work;
put the IP in an entity (LLC recommended); collaborator agreement template ready.
**Done when:** registration receipt exists, entity decision made, search results filed.
**Judged on:** roadmap Phase 0 gate (not a creative step — no dimensions).

## Step 3 — Complete the Pitch Package

**Goal:** a stranger absorbs the whole show in 15 minutes and can retell it in 2.
**Who:** me (drafting), you (approval passes).
**Tasks:** pitch deck (10–12 pages); Fathom one-page rules card; pilot script draft 2
polish; package assembly (deck + pilot + beat sheet + key art placeholder slots).
**Done when:** three cold readers retell the premise accurately after one pass.
**Judged on:** dimensions 1, 4, 8 + the playground test.

## Step 4 — Lock the Look

**Goal:** UNDERTOW has a face — characters, palettes, and five key art pieces on model.
**Who:** me (AI look-dev generation from the visdev briefs), you (taste calls),
a commissioned artist later (finals).
**Tasks:** generate look-dev batches per character + the five key pieces; you pick
winners; iterate to a locked style guide; shortlist human artists for finals.
**Done when:** a one-page visual style guide exists with approved reference images for
all six mains + Kemar, the palette strip, and one hero key art.
**Judged on:** dimension 2 (+6 for body/sport truth in poses).

## Step 5 — Manga Chapter 1

**Goal:** the proof medium starts. Episode 1 "Anchor," re-broken for vertical scroll.
**Who:** me (chapter script/panel breakdown), you (artist choice + budget), artist
(pages).
**Tasks:** re-break the pilot into webtoon panels (depth = the scroll axis); artist
outreach with the locked style guide; letter + publish plan (Webtoon Canvas / Jump+ /
self-drop).
**Done when:** Chapter 1 is live on a platform with a publish cadence committed.
**Judged on:** dimensions 1, 2, 3, 10.

## Step 6 — First Teaser

**Goal:** the sinking scene as a 60–90s animated teaser — the IP's first viral shot.
**Who:** me (board + AI animatic), you (voice/music calls, posting).
**Tasks:** board the Episode 1 sinking sequence (spec already in visdev §6); generate
the animatic; sound pass (heartbeat score, the one vast tone); cut vertical + wide.
**Done when:** teaser is posted on at least two channels with the chapter link in bio.
**Judged on:** dimensions 2, 3 (works with sound off AND sound alone).

## Step 7 — The Audience Engine

**Goal:** turn output into a compounding fanbase.
**Who:** you (voice of the project), me (content pipeline).
**Tasks:** home base + three feeds; Discord at chapter 1; weekly lore-drop cadence from
the codexes (power-scaling content is the engagement engine); water-safety partnership
outreach list; track the roadmap Phase 3 benchmarks.
**Done when:** four consecutive weeks of on-cadence chapters + drops, with retention
data collected.
**Judged on:** roadmap Phase 3 benchmarks.

## Step 8 — Tradition-Keepers Round

**Goal:** every House's cultural material built with people who live those traditions —
before wide visibility makes fixes expensive.
**Who:** you (engagement + budget), me (briefs per House, from the codex flags).
**Tasks:** engage keepers for the Houses on screen early (Japan/ama, Jamaica/Caribbean,
Polynesia first); theology read on the Bible thread; fold every note back through the
gauntlet as logged amendments.
**Done when:** all Houses appearing in published chapters have been through their
keepers' hands.
**Judged on:** dimensions 5, 7.

## Step 9 — The Deal

**Goal:** traction converts into an adaptation path.
**Who:** you (the room), me (materials + data story).
**Tasks:** update the package with real numbers; rank the three routes (platform →
committee / streamer original / crowdfunded pilot) against actual traction; take
meetings; protect bible-level canon approval rights in any term sheet.
**Done when:** signed development deal or funded pilot — the roadmap Phase 4 gate.
**Judged on:** everything — this is where the whole gauntlet pays off.

---

**Standing rule:** steps ship in order, but canon never sleeps — new elements you name
keep flowing into [UNDERTOW-ELEMENTS.md](UNDERTOW-ELEMENTS.md) through the gauntlet at
