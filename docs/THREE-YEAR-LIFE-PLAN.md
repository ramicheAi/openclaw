## Part 2 — What's holding you down (you asked; here it is straight)

1. **Scattered fire.** Software, music, movies, trading, off-grid homesteading,
   multiple women, many repos. You have builder's energy but it's split eight
   ways. Nothing compounds when everything gets 12% of you. The #1 change in
   this plan: **one income engine gets 80% of your work hours for Year 1.**
   Everything else is scheduled, small, or paused.

2. **The dating apps.** You're volume-dating: several apps, many dates, no
   selection criteria, no destination. That spends the exact three resources
   you need for a wife — time, money, and sexual discipline — and it selects
   *against* the woman you described, because the woman you described is
   generally not living on Hinge. The apps also feed the lust loop directly.
   They go. (Protocol in Part 5.)

3. **The lust habit.** Masturbation/porn is a habit loop, not a moral identity.
   But left running, it quietly drains the aggression you need for business and
   the clarity you need to choose a wife, and it keeps you tolerating loneliness
   instead of fixing it. It gets a real protocol (Part 5), not just willpower
   and shame cycles.

4. **No numbers.** "Financially free" and "set for life" are feelings until
   they're numbers. You currently don't have (written down): monthly burn,
   savings rate, net worth, a freedom number, a land budget. Month 1 fixes this.
   You cannot render what you haven't specified — you know this from software.

5. **Reward-first thinking.** The Porsche, the house, the land are all
   *outputs*. When outputs get bought before the engine exists, they become
   debt and anchors. In this plan every reward is gated behind a milestone the
   engine already paid for.

6. **"Burn the system" energy pointed the wrong way.** You're right that a lot
   of what school taught was conditioning, and right to want your own rules.
   But here's the move the truly free make: you exit the system by **owning
   things inside it** — land title, a paid-off well and solar array, a business,
   cash flow, no debt. Land title is a legal instrument. Solar is bought with
   dollars. Rejecting the tools keeps you *in* the system (renting, dependent);
   mastering the tools is what gets you out. Channel the distrust into
   ownership, not opting out.

7. **Trading as a hoped shortcut.** Trading is real but it is a *skill with a
   long apprenticeship*, and most people fund other traders' freedom while
   learning. In this plan trading exists — with strict guardrails (Part 4) —
   but it is **not** the freedom engine. Your agent-systems skill is.

None of these are character flaws. They're open loops. The plan closes them.

## Part 4 — The money system (freedom, by the numbers)

Do this in Month 1, before anything else:

1. **Know your burn.** Track every dollar for 30 days. Get the real monthly
   number.
2. **Define the freedom number.** Freedom = investments ≈ **25× annual
   spending** (the 4% rule), *plus* the land/home paid off. If you'll spend
   $6k/mo as a family, that's $72k/yr → **$1.8M invested + paid-off property.**
   Your number will differ — compute it, write it in memory-core, review
   monthly.
3. **Order of operations** (do not reorder; the order is the strategy):
   1. 1 month expenses in cash → 2. kill all consumer debt → 3. 6 months
   expenses (your "no one owns me" fund) → 4. invest 40–60% of income:
   boring index funds as the base → 5. **land fund** → 6. play capital.
4. **The land play (Year 2):** buy the **land before the house**. Raw/rural
   acreage with water rights and good sun runs roughly $3k–$15k/acre depending
   on region (research regions in Year 1: water table, zoning, building codes,
   distance to an airport — you want adventure *and* self-sufficiency). Buying
   land first locks the dream in place for $50–150k while the business scales,
   and you can drill the well, install solar, and build in phases with cash.
5. **Trading rules (non-negotiable):** paper trade 6 months before real money.
   Then risk ≤1% of account per trade, and the account is capped at ≤10% of
   net worth. Journal every trade. If down 20%, stop for 90 days and review.
   Trading money is *play capital* (step 6), never rent, never the land fund.
6. **The Porsche gate:** buy it in cash when **monthly business revenue has
   covered 2× the purchase price in cumulative profit** and the land fund is
   fully on schedule. A clean used Cayman/911 is $45–90k. Earned that way, it's
   a trophy. Any other way, it's a leash. (Nothing wrong with wanting it —
   wanting it is fuel. Gate it.)

## Part 5 — The man (faith, lust, body)

### Scripture
You're in Numbers — chapter ~118 of 1,189. **Three chapters a day finishes the
entire Bible in under one year.** That's 15–20 minutes each morning, before
the phone. Pair it with one written line: "what does this change about today?"
— that's the "apply it to my life" part, captured in your own system. Read
Proverbs twice on the way through; it is the operating manual for everything
in Parts 3 and 4. And take Leviticus the way you've been taking it — order,
cleanliness, discipline, holiness in the daily and physical — while letting
the whole book (you'll hit the Gospels around winter) show you where grace
fits, so discipline doesn't curdle into self-condemnation.

### Lust protocol (habit engineering, not willpower)
- **Remove triggers:** delete the apps (below), blocker on all devices with a
  friend holding the password, phone charges outside the bedroom, no screens
  in bed. You design guardrails for agents so they can't misfire — design your
  environment the same way. Don't rely on the model to behave; remove the tool.
- **Replace, don't just resist:** the urge is energy. Route it: pushups to
  failure, cold shower, walk, work sprint. Every single time, same routine.
- **Track streaks** in your own system (it's private, it's yours, and you
  already built streak-capable memory). Relapse = data, not identity: note the
  trigger, patch the environment, continue. A 90-day clean run will change
  your baseline more than anything else on this page.
- **Tell one man.** Accountability that can't be silently ignored. If you find
  a church (below), find him there.

### The wife (get off the apps, get into rooms)
- **Delete all dating apps by end of Month 1.** Volume dating is the opposite
  of selection. If any current connection genuinely matches the list below,
  pursue *her* intentionally and drop the rest; otherwise, clean cut.
- **Go where she already is:** a solid church you attend weekly (this solves
  faith community, male accountability, *and* meeting a God-fearing woman —
  one move, three goals), plus real-world rooms: fitness, dance/music scenes,
  service/volunteering.
- **Write the actual list, both directions:** her — peaceful, respectful,
  supportive, virtuous, smart, funny, God-fearing, loves you for who you are,
  wants kids, can live on land away from the city. You — the man that woman is
  praying for is disciplined, financially directed, sexually self-controlled,
  and spiritually consistent. **This entire plan is you becoming him.** Courtship
  with intention: exclusive, purposeful, meeting families, honest about the
  homestead vision early. Timeline that fits your window: meet in Year 1,
  engaged in Year 2, married Year 3, kids right on schedule.

### Body
- Strength 4×/week. Own the pushup: run a 30-day progression to **50 clean
  consecutive pushups**, then keep it as a daily floor. A future homestead
  needs a strong owner — fences, wells, and firewood don't care about your
  net worth.
- Eat clean per your convictions (whole food, unprocessed; keep the
  Leviticus-informed standard you've adopted). Sleep 7–8h. The 10pm phone
  cutoff serves the lust protocol, the sleep, and the 6am scripture all at once.
