# UNDERTOW — Chapter 1: "Anchor"
### Manga/Webtoon Adaptation (QC-passed) — from [UNDERTOW-EP1-SCRIPT.md](UNDERTOW-EP1-SCRIPT.md)

Format note: vertical-scroll webtoon, full color per visdev §1 palette-by-depth. Panels
are numbered sequentially and meant to be read top-to-bottom in one continuous canvas per
scene, with gutter width doing narrative work (a wide gap = a held breath). Two sequences
are called out as **LONG VERTICAL DESCENT** panels — the reader physically scrolls down
through the water with Kai. This is the chapter's structural thesis: the medium becomes
the drowning.

Palette key used throughout (visdev §1): **Chlorine Day** (cyan `#59C7E3`/tile white
`#F2F7F7`/sun gold `#FFD98A`), **Green-Gold** (sea green `#2E8B74`/dawn gold `#E8B84B`),
**Violet Fade** (deep violet `#3B2E63`/steel blue `#2F4A6E`), **Bioluminal** (abyss navy
`#0B1026`/glyph teal `#3EF2C4`), **Absence** (true black `#05060A`).

## COVER PANEL (chapter splash, precedes Panel 1)

**Visual:** Vertical one-sheet composition (per visdev key art #2, "Deep Calls to Deep,"
scaled down to a chapter cover). Top third: a harbor and a small house, warm window-light,
Green-Gold dusk. The image runs downward without a hard panel break — Violet Fade, then
Bioluminal — and at the very bottom, small and glowing, a boy's silhouette descends past
bark-glyphs spiraling down an impossibly vast trunk shape. Chapter logo **UNDERTOW / 逆流**
lockup sits in the Chlorine-to-Green-Gold band near the top, white on the dark water below
it so it reads at thumbnail size.
**Format:** Single full-bleed vertical canvas, taller than any interior panel — this is the
piece that has to sell the series on a thumbnail.
**Text:** Logo only. No dialogue on the cover — let the color drop do the pitching.

  plus one softer bonus descent for the end-card beat (Panels 71–73), echoing the cover.
