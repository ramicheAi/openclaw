# UNDERTOW — Chapter 2: "Dry Land"
### Manga/Webtoon Adaptation (continues directly from [UNDERTOW-MANGA-CH1.md](UNDERTOW-MANGA-CH1.md)) — episode beats per [UNDERTOW-S1-BEATSHEET.md](UNDERTOW-S1-BEATSHEET.md), Episode 2: "Dry Land."

Format note: vertical-scroll webtoon, full color per visdev §1 palette-by-depth, matching
Chapter 1's conventions exactly. Panels are numbered sequentially within this chapter and
read top-to-bottom in one continuous canvas per scene, gutter width doing narrative work
(a wide gap = a held breath). This chapter has no LONG VERTICAL DESCENT sequences by
design — its whole thesis is the title: nobody gets wet this chapter. The one held-breath
signature panel (62) is staged as this chapter's answer to Chapter 1's pool-floor splash —
same stillness, this time chosen, dry, and watched over by one steady friend instead of a
trance and a crowd.

Character-design lock: Kai remains drawn per his visdev sheet — a mixed Black boy, deep
brown skin, tightly-coiled blue-black hair falling toward his eyes, his mother's shell-cord
bracelet always on his wrist, ordinary dark brown eyes on dry land (the Undertow glow does
not appear anywhere in this chapter — he never submerges). His heritage is shown, never
stated, and no panel dialogue remarks on it. Bo (heavyset, sun-weathered, cream cable-knit
sweater/club jacket), Mirei (bob, prescription glasses always on-face, navy tracksuit,
MANAGER armband, stopwatch), and Gouda (graying topknot, heavy jaw, ordinary dark eyes,
rumpled tracksuit) hold their Chapter 1 designs exactly, per
[UNDERTOW-CHARACTER-CANON.json](UNDERTOW-CHARACTER-CANON.json).

Palette key used throughout (visdev §1, unchanged from Chapter 1): **Chlorine Day** (cyan
`#59C7E3`/tile white `#F2F7F7`/sun gold `#FFD98A`), **Green-Gold** (sea green `#2E8B74`/dawn
gold `#E8B84B`), **Violet Fade** (deep violet `#3B2E63`/steel blue `#2F4A6E`),
**Bioluminal** (abyss navy `#0B1026`/glyph teal `#3EF2C4`), **Absence** (true black
`#05060A`). This chapter lives almost entirely in Chlorine Day and Green-Gold — the
"normal world" palette — with one short Bioluminal-adjacent, sepia-and-blue Old Build
cold-open bridge (visdev §5's aesthetic) bracketing the start and end.

## Chapter stats
- **77 numbered panels** (+ chapter cover splash), matching Chapter 1's structural grammar:
  a cold-institutional bridge → three Acts → a warm home-table button → a post-credits
  hook, minus Chapter 1's Long Vertical Descents by design (this chapter is dry land, on
  purpose, until the season is ready to send Kai back in).
- No Undertow eye-glow anywhere in this chapter — Kai never submerges, so the rule is never
  at risk of being broken.
- Signature/answer panel: **#62**, the dock-held-breath image, built as a direct, warmer
  inversion of Chapter 1's pool-floor splash (#46).
- The Water's Rules (buddy system, no hyperventilation, never alone) are dramatized
  explicitly at Panels 32–33 and shown as lived habit at 58 and 61 — no violation occurs,
  so no consequence beat was needed this chapter.
- Verbatim lines carried in from the beat sheet's intent: Bo's "sinks outrank quits," the
  buddy-system recitation, Mirei's silent notebook, the Prefectural four-lengths clock, and
  the grandmother's table exchange (echoing Chapter 1's Jonah beat without repeating it).
- Bridging device: the "ISOZAKI — CLASSIFIED, 15Y" drawer, opened at Panel 4 and reviewed
  at Panel 77, ties directly to the Lore Codex's dating of Daigo's Warden-designation
  (15 years before present) and keeps the hydrophone-signal mystery moving without
  resolving it ahead of schedule.
