CONCEPT

A Tau. A scale. A single iconic shape.

The mark is a brass capital T — for Themis, for Tau, for the simplest possible scale. The vertical stem is plumb: truth is fixed. The horizontal crossbar is tilted seven degrees: weight is being applied.

The left end of the beam is a solid block — grounded, verified evidence. The right end is a dashed outline — floating, pending. A small ink dot at the join marks the fulcrum, the decision point.

At 16 pixels the dashed weight disappears. The right block thickens. The mark reduces to a single tilted T — readable from anywhere in the room.
