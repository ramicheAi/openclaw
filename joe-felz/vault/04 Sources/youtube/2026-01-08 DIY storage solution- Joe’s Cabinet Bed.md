# DIY storage solution: Joe’s Cabinet Bed

## Description

I did not have room for a closet in my new Costa Rican Casita, and noticed a huge unused space under the bed where we sleep. So I decided to build a cabinet bed, which consists of these materials:
4- 75”x 8” floor runners 
7- 54”x 8” top slats 
8- 26” pieces of 1/2” metal conduit
8- 23 1/2” pieces of 3/4” conduit
Drawer consists of:
2- 66”x 4” sides
1-22” x 4” front
1- 24” 4” back
1-26 1/2” x 6” drawer face

It took me about five hours to build the whole thing and it cost less than 50 bucks total in materials .

Here in Costa Rica I only have four shirts and pairs of shorts so everything fits easily under my bed and I don’t have any wasted closet.
