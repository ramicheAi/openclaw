# 2026-06-21 Father’s Day, for me, isn’t just about fathers

## Caption

Father’s Day, for me, isn’t just about fathers.

It’s about the mothers who stepped into both roles when a father wasn’t there. The ones who carried the weight of two parents, often without recognition, because their children needed them to.

It’s about the sisters who stood beside their sisters in the delivery room when no one else did. The sisters, aunts, grandparents, and family members who showed up for birthdays, holidays, school events, and the everyday moments that matter when someone else chose not to.

It’s about the men who became role models for children who needed one, even when they had no obligation to do so. The coaches, uncles, mentors, family friends, and neighbors who quietly helped shape a life simply by being present.

It’s about the fathers who show up every single day—through the sleepless nights, the sacrifices, the worries, and the responsibilities. The fathers who have had to carry both parental roles when a mother was absent.

It’s also about the fathers who can’t always be physically present because they’re out working long hours, making sacrifices, and doing everything they can to provide a better life for their children.

And it’s about the young men who were forced to grow up too soon, stepping into adult responsibilities and fatherly roles long before they should have had to.

The truth is, raising children has never been a one-person job. It takes love. It takes sacrifice. It takes people showing up for each other.

It takes a whole motherfuckin’ village these days, y’all.

Happy Father’s Day to everyone who stepped up when it mattered. ❤️ #fathers #family #community #thekidswillbealright #joeandalx
