I’ve been fishing my entire life but never like today…

I am in a traditional fishing village on the coastline of Colombia where many of the fisherman still catch their meal every day. The coastline is gorgeous, with massive rock formations in multiple different hues of grey and blue.

How “traditional” is it here? Well:

1. The guys here do not use poles, just wooden circles with thick line on it that they throw out and pull in by hand (pic 3).

2. We are in a tiny little boat with a motor that cut out about & had to be fixed when we first started (pic that they are currently working on right now (pic 6).

3. We are using spark plugs for sinkers, which really tickles me (pic 10).

My inner redneck is smiling from ear to ear. There is high value in being low class 🇨🇴🎣❤️ #joefelz #experience #colombia #fishing #traditional
