## Caption

Life hasn’t been as glamorous as the Costa Rican jungle lately. 

Over the last month in Augusta we went from monkeys, rivers and our biggest daily stressor being a leaky manguera…to 12-14 hour work days EVERY day, trying to juggle home renovations, court cases, family dynamics, media, admin work, and somehow trying to still remember to eat, breathe and sometimes shower. 

We’re tired, we’re beat up- some days not even sure which direction we’re headed- but we’re still alive. 

First, I wanna thank every single one of you who’s stuck with us - from bibles, to vanlife, ancient artifacts, homesteading, renovations, solar etc. We know we’re all over the place. We don’t get to respond to every message but read more than you realize. Your stories, struggles, achievements and resilience have helped carry us through and give us the strength to get back up in the morning. 

Second- and this one’s important- I know the whole lot of us are fighting battles right now. Financially, family, health, uncertainty. I don’t know what sick chapter of life this is but it’s for the birds. BUT- there is light at the end of the tunnel and if you can’t see it yet- keep walking. One foot in front of the other, one day at a time, one problem at a time. 

Strength isn’t winning, strength is refusing to quit. 

And if I’m wrong and there isn’t light at the end of the tunnel, we die and it’s over and doesn’t matter anyways. 

Hang in there guys. We got this😂💪🏻🤍 #mindset #lifebelifin #strength #community #joeandalx
