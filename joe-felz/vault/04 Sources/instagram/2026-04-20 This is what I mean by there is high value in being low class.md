This is what I mean by there is high value in being low class.

I had never traditionally fished in my life, but that changed yesterday. @igniteselfmastery & I headed out with two local fisherman on a tiny boat with a motor that worked in fits and starts to catch our meal. Full video on YT @joefelz if you wanna see the whole thing.

We had no fishing poles, lures, or fishfinder, just waves vibes and a ton of fun. I have a newfound respect for these humble fisherman and the work ethic they embody to do this daily to feed themselves.

Although few people do it in 2026, I can confidently tell you that a meal tastes better when you catch it yourself. It was a great day for all, especially the street cat we blessed with our hard earned fish scraps. Viva Colombia 🇨🇴🎣😋 #joefelz #fishing #colombia #experiences #travel
