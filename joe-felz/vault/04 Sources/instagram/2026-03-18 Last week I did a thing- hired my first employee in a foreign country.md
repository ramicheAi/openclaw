José was referred to me as one of the best agricultural minds in this area with intimate knowledge of plant cultivation & animal husbandry. Given that he knows way more than me, José is basically his own boss. He tells me what we’re gonna do & I listen, not the other way around. He also told me this is his DREAM JOB- his last bosses had a literal overseer who watched all workers all 8 hours daily.
